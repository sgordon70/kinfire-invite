Subscription

  • The person who starts a family pays for it. Everyone who joins with the invite code is covered. A family can have up to 25 people.
  • Plans are monthly or annual. New subscribers may get a free trial. The price and trial length are shown before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules.
  • If the subscription ends, nobody in the family can record new answers, but everyone can still listen to past ones.

You own what you record. You give us permission to store your recordings and share them with the people in your family, which is the only reason we have them. You can delete them at any time (see the privacy policy).

Using Kinfire fairly

Only record yourself, or people who are fine with being recorded. Do not use Kinfire to harass anyone or to share anything illegal. Share your invite code only with people you want in your family. We may remove a family that breaks these rules.
