Why we built it

Most family stories get lost by accident

The stuff you actually want to know about the people you love usually comes out once, at a holiday dinner, and never gets asked again. Waiting for that moment to happen naturally doesn't work. Handing someone a stack of memoir prompts and asking them to write their life story doesn't work either — it feels like homework, so it sits unfinished.

Kinfire is built to be a five-minute habit instead of a someday intention. One question, one voice memo, once a week. Small enough that it actually happens.

How it works

One ritual, every Sunday

No feeds to scroll, no daily nagging — just a quiet weekly rhythm.

1

Two matched questions land each week

One looking back for the grandparent, one looking forward for the grandkid — same theme, different angle.

2

3

4

It quietly builds into an archive

52 weeks a year, sequenced from light to meaningful — a growing private record of both your voices.

The difference

Not another memoir project

Most family story apps hand one person — almost always the grandparent — a stack of prompts to answer alone. Everyone else reads it later, if the book ever gets finished. That's homework, not a conversation.

Typical story apps

  • One person answers — usually the grandparent
  • Weekly prompt → a book, eventually
  • The kid reads it later, if at all
  • Output: a future memoir, someday

Kinfire

  • Both people answer — every single week
  • Weekly question → a Sunday audio digest
  • Both of you hear each other, right away
  • Output: the exchange itself, weekly

FAQ

Good to know

Does it matter if we have different phones?

No. One invite code works whether your family's on iPhone, Android, or a mix of both.

Is this private?

Yes. Recordings stay inside your family — no public sharing, no ads, no analytics selling your data. You can permanently delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What if we miss a week?

Nothing is lost. The next week's question shows up regardless, and there's no streak to protect — this isn't meant to feel like a chore.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, anytime, no long-term commitment beyond the period you're already in.
