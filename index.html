What it is

Not another group chat

Kinfire asks your family one question a week. Each person records a short answer whenever it suits them. Nobody is waiting on a reply, and there is no thread to keep up with. The answers stay in the app so you can play them again next month or in ten years.

Answer on your own time

Record in the car, after dinner, whenever. Nobody sees "typing..." and nobody is left on read.

Their actual voices

Text is fine, but hearing your mom laugh at her own story is better.

Everything is kept

Every week goes into your family's archive. Scroll back to any week and play it again.

Easy for older relatives

The Elder screen is one big button and large text. No menus. Tap, talk, done.

iPhone and Android together

Same question for the whole family each week, whatever phone anyone has.

Wherever everyone lives

Like a Sunday supper for the people who can't be at the table. Different towns, time zones or countries, same question, and everyone hears each other.

How it works

Three steps

One person starts the family and sends the invite link. Everyone else taps it and they're in. Nothing to type.

One topic, two versions

1. A new question on Sunday

Each week has one topic and two versions of the question: one for the Elders in your family and one for everyone else. Same topic, a slightly different angle. Most weeks are about pranks, bad haircuts and strange food. A few go deeper.

Elder screen

2. Everyone records an answer

Elders get one big button and large type. Tap it, talk, tap again. A second button plays what the rest of the family said. Everyone else gets the full app with the question, the answers so far and a record button.

Ledger

3. Listen any time

Play the whole week's answers back to back, or pick one person. Every past week stays in the Ledger, your family's archive.

Why we made this

We wanted to hear our dad's voice again

After our dad died, we realized we had almost no recordings of him, and a long list of things we never asked. With everyone busy and spread out, the stories weren't getting told. Kinfire is our way of keeping them going, for the people who are still here.

FAQ

Good to know

Does it matter if we have different phones?

No. One invite code works whether your family's on iPhone, Android, or a mix of both.

Who counts as an Elder?

Whoever you want. A grandparent, an older parent, a great-aunt. Elders get the simple one-button screen and their own version of the question.

Is this private?

Yes. Only people in your family can hear your answers. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. You can delete your answers, or leave the family and delete everything about you, from Settings. Privacy policy.

How does someone join?

Send them the invite from Kinfire's Settings. They tap the link, install Kinfire if they don't have it, and their family comes up when they open the app. Nobody has to type a code.

How often will Kinfire notify me?

Once a week, Sunday at 6 PM, when the new question is ready. That's the only notification Kinfire sends.

Can I remove someone from our family?

Yes. Whoever started the family can remove anyone in Settings, for example an old copy of an Elder whose phone was replaced. They can always join again with the invite.

What if we miss a week?

Nothing happens. The next question still shows up on Sunday, and there is no streak to keep.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How many people can join?

Up to 25 people per family. Anyone in the family can share the invite code.

Does everyone in the family have to pay?

No. One subscription covers the whole family. The person who starts the family pays, and everyone who joins with the code is included.
