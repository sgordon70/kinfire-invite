What Kinfire actually is

Not a group text. A weekly voice exchange.

Kinfire asks your whole family one matched question every week. Everyone records a short voice answer on their own time, no thread, no notifications piling up, no one waiting on a reply. Sunday night, every answer gets put together into one digest anyone can play whenever they want. It's not a memoir project either: nobody's handed a stack of prompts to fill out alone.

Individual, not a thread

Everyone answers on their own, in their own time. No group chat to keep up with, no "reply all," no notifications piling up.

Listen whenever, no pressure

Nobody's waiting on you. Play it during a commute, at dinner, whenever it actually works for you.

Saved, not scrolled past

Every answer and every digest sticks around. Come back and listen again next month or next year.

Real insight into each other

You hear how each person in your family actually thinks and answers, not just what they post.

Building your family's history

Every week adds to a growing archive of real voices, still there for your family years from now.

Genuinely easy

One glowing button for grandparents, nothing to set up for anyone else. Answering takes under a minute.

How it works

One ritual, every Sunday

No feed to scroll, no daily nagging, no homework. Just a quick weekly rhythm everyone actually looks forward to.

Family view

1. Two matched questions land each week

Same theme, two angles, one for grandparents, one for the rest of the family. Most weeks are playful, a few get more thoughtful. You each see the other's answer and record your own right alongside it.

Grandparent view

2. The grandparent view is one button

Whoever's in the grandparent role gets one giant glowing button and huge text. No menus, no scrolling, nothing to figure out. Tap, talk, done.

3. Sunday night, it's all put together for you

Every answer from that week is automatically combined into one recording, with a little chime, that anyone in the family can play. Week after week, it builds into a growing collection you can always go back and listen to.

Sample questions

Mostly fun. Occasionally thoughtful.

Most weeks are about pranks, weird food, bad haircuts, and cheesy crushes. A few weeks ask something a bit more thoughtful. That's the mix, on purpose.

"What's the best prank you ever pulled, or had pulled on you?"
"What's the weirdest food combo you actually love?"

"What's the cheesiest thing you ever did to impress a crush?"
"What's the worst gift you ever got and had to fake liking?"
"What's the funniest thing you got away with as a kid?"
Why we made this

We built the app we wished we'd had

We started Kinfire after realizing how many ordinary questions we'd never actually asked our own family, and how easy it is to let that slide for years. It's not only about the big, thoughtful stuff. Most weeks it's just a good excuse to hear everyone's voice, corny stories included.

FAQ

Good to know

Does it matter if we have different phones?

No. One invite code works whether your family's on iPhone, Android, or a mix of both.

Is this private?

Yes. Recordings stay inside your family. No public sharing, no ads, no analytics selling your data. Delete everything permanently from inside the app at any time.

What if we miss a week?

Nothing is lost. The next week's question shows up regardless, and there's no streak to protect.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes, anytime, no long-term commitment beyond the period you're already in.

How many people can join?

Up to 25 people in one family circle, as many grandparents, parents, and kids as you need. Anyone already in can forward the invite code to bring in someone else.

Does everyone in the family have to pay?

No, just one subscription covers the whole family circle. Whoever sets it up pays once, and everyone who joins with the invite code is included at no extra cost.
